Widget 4: Same ERF, Different Models

Pick a target model, which plays the role of the data-generating model. The page finds the binomial, GPCM, and GRM that best reproduce its expected response function, then shows how differently each one spreads probability across categories.

How to read it. The target’s curves are solid; the selected candidate’s are dashed. Every candidate matches the target’s ERF exactly or nearly so, so the ERF panel always shows two overlapping curves. The difference shows up in the category curves and in the KL column of the table.

Try this. (1) Binomial target, nearest GRM: the GRM matches the ERF by tying all its thresholds and puts nearly all probability on the lowest and highest categories (see the bars). (2) GRM target, nearest GPCM: the ERF matches exactly, but the category curves differ. (3) GPCM target, nearest GRM: here even the ERF match is only approximate.

Why the exact matches happen. The binomial with a logistic link is a GPCM, so a GPCM can always reproduce it. More surprisingly, every GRM ERF is exactly a GPCM ERF with the same slope: Σk logistic(a(θ−βk)) is the derivative in η = aθ of log Πk(1 + eη−aβk), and that product equals Σk ekη ek(x), with xk = e−aβk and ek the elementary symmetric polynomials. That is a GPCM normalizing constant with steps τk = log(ek−1/ek). The matching GPCM’s categories follow a Poisson-binomial distribution (a sum of K−1 independent logistic Bernoullis), not the GRM’s. The reverse fails: a GPCM ERF is a GRM ERF only when that polynomial has all real roots. A GRM with tied thresholds has exactly the binomial’s ERF.

Details. The binomial candidate uses f(θ) = ERF/(K−1), so it matches any ERF exactly. The GPCM and GRM candidates have linear links. The GPCM is always exact (the target itself, the binomial’s GPCM form, or the GRM→GPCM identity). The GRM is exact for a GRM target; otherwise it is fitted by Nelder–Mead to minimize ERF error, weighting θ by N(0,1). To hold the ERF fixed and vary the threshold structure directly, see the fixed-ERF threshold explorer.
